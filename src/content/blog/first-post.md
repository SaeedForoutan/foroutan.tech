This is the first post on this site. I've wanted a place of my own on the internet for a while, somewhere to share what I'm thinking about — my insights, my ideas, and whatever I happen to be discovering day to day. Some of that will be about my career and my field. A fair amount of it won't be.

For now everything goes into one pile. In a few months, once there's enough here to be worth sorting, I'll split it into categories: AI in one, my life outside work in another, and a third for the miscellaneous things that don't fit either. I'd also like to add my research, the practical projects I'm working on, and eventually a proper portfolio.

A YouTube channel is somewhere on that list too. If it happens, I'll connect it to the site.

## About me

I recently graduated from Sharif University of Technology with a Master of Science in Artificial Intelligence. After that I moved to the Czech Republic to continue at VŠB – Technical University of Ostrava. Ostrava is a calm, quiet city near the Polish border, and so far it suits me well.

More to come on my hobbies, my day to day, and the work I'm doing.
